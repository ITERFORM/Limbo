# Dense 27B and the int8 decode path (2026-11-26)

Machine: RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB · Ryzen 7 3800X · PCIe Gen3 x8. The X11 desktop shares the card: its VRAM use decides which
drafter the fit can afford and its GPU load moves every timing below (single runs, not machine-gated).

Code: main at 5c41046 against the build before each change, run back to back. Decode figures are greedy, one request,
`scripts/serve/bench.sh <model> 1 400` (bench/prompts/code.txt) unless a prompt is named; the fit is `limbo -hf`'s own.

## The changes measured here

- DFlash2 drafters kept in their GGUF formats; the zoo offers Q6_K / Q4_K_M / Q2_K and the fit takes the best that fits.
- Decode steps and verify windows of 1..16 rows on the int8 fragment kernel (one weight decode serves every row).
- Dense prefill on a pipelined int8 kernel (weights and half super-block activations double buffered).
- Decode attention over Q8/Q4 pools on the tensor cores (groups of at most 8 heads per KV head).

| verify window of 8 rows at 32,768 positions, q4 K/V | 90.2 ms (scalar multi-row attention) | 75.8 ms |
| decode, code.txt, fit: ngram + DFlash2 drafter, q4 K/V at 57,344 positions | | 46.2 tok/s |
| decode, code.txt, fit: MTP + ngram + Q2_K drafter, q4 K/V at 49,152 positions | | 37.6 tok/s |

The two code.txt fits differ only in what the desktop left free: 2,741 MiB above the reserve gave the first, 2,493 MiB
the second (the planner puts the drafter first and keeps at least 48K positions, so the smaller budget takes the Q2_K
drafter beside the MTP block). Plain row 60.1 ms in the first, 63.9 ms in the second.

Prefill planes: with 2 planes the 48 greedy tokens after a 4096-token prefill were identical to the token-by-token
reference; with 1 plane (725 tok/s) they were not.

## Exactness

- Prefill: `bench_prefill --golden-check` against files saved with the previous kernel: `0 of 248320 logits differ` for
  Signal (2 planes), the IQ3_XXS 27B (2 planes) and A3B (3 planes).
- Decode: a step and every window size compute a row with the same bits (cuda_dense_test, cuda_decode_kv_test,
  cuda_decode_attn_test over f16, Q8 and Q4 pools, 6-head groups included; worst 8.6e-7 against the CPU).
- `make test`: 28 of 28.
